# Progress belongs to the learner and the lesson, not the purchase

Lesson progress and video reached are recorded against the learner and each lesson, not against a purchase. A repurchase therefore resumes where the learner stopped. We rejected scoping progress to the purchase, because every new purchase would reset it and the admin would lose the history behind a decision.

Consequence: the admin's progress view counts everything the learner has done on the course across all of their purchases.
